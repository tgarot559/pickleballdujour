Pickleball glossary

In shortThe five must-know words: kitchen (the non-volley zone), dink (a soft shot landing in the opponent's kitchen), drop (the soft third shot), stacking (a doubles positioning tactic) and DUPR (the international rating).
ATP (Around The Post)
A shot that goes around the net post rather than over the net. Perfectly legal, and spectacular.
Banger
A player who hits hard on every ball. Works against beginners, much less against a good defender.
Dink
A soft, low shot played from the kitchen line to land in the opponent's kitchen. The heart of the game.
Drive
A hard, flat shot, usually from the back of the court.
Drop (third shot drop)
The rally's third shot, played softly into the opponent's kitchen so the serving team can move up to the net.
DUPR
Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating, the international rating system, from 2.0 to above 7.0.
Erne
A volley hit by jumping over the corner of the kitchen or from outside the court beside it. Legal as long as you don't touch the zone.
Foot fault
Touching the kitchen while volleying, or stepping on the baseline when serving.
Kitchen
The non-volley zone, 7 ft on each side of the net. You can enter it, but not volley from it.
Lob
A high ball over an opponent at the net.
Nasty Nelson
A serve aimed at the receiver's partner standing near the kitchen: if it hits them, the point goes to the server. Legal, but not popular.
Poach
In doubles, crossing over to intercept a ball meant for your partner.
Rally scoring
Every rally wins a point, whoever serves. Tested in the US, with a decision announced for 2027.
Reset
A soft ball played under pressure to slow the game and get back to neutral.
Server 1, server 2
The serving order within a doubles team, called as the third number of the score.
Side out
When the serve passes to the other team.
Speed-up
A sudden acceleration, often during a dink rally, to surprise the opponent.
Stacking
A doubles tactic where partners always keep the same side of the court, switching after the serve.
Transition zone
The area between the baseline and the kitchen. Move through it; don't stay in it.
Two-bounce rule
The return of serve and the third shot must bounce before being played.
Volley
Hitting the ball before it bounces. Not allowed from the kitchen.

Frequently asked questions

What is a dink in pickleball?

A soft, low shot played from the kitchen line that lands in the opponent's kitchen, so it can't be attacked on the volley.

What is the kitchen in pickleball?

The non-volley zone: 7 ft (2.13 m) on each side of the net, where you can't volley.

What is an erne?

A volley played around the kitchen, by jumping or from outside the court. Legal if you don't touch the zone.
