Find a pickleball coach or class in France

In shortTo take pickleball lessons in France, search your town in the directory below: it separates venues that advertise lessons from places where you can simply play. Otherwise, ask the nearest tennis club: over 1,000 FFT clubs offer pickleball, often with taster sessions.

    Group or private lessons?

    To start, group lessons are best: you learn the rules, the serve and the dink while playing doubles straight away. Private lessons help later, to fix a specific shot or prepare for a tournament.

    Who can teach?

    In France, teaching a sport for pay requires a recognised qualification (such as the state tennis diploma) and a sports instructor card. Volunteers can lead unpaid sessions in their club.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does a pickleball lesson cost?

    Prices depend on the town, the club and the format (group, private, camp). Associations often offer a yearly membership that includes coached sessions.

    Do I need a licence to take lessons?

    FFT-affiliated clubs usually ask for a licence with membership. Taster sessions often don't.
