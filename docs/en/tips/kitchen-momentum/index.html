Watch your momentum after a volley

In shortVolley just in front of the line, then step into the kitchen, even after the rally ends: that's a fault. Momentum counts.

It's the most frustrating fault: the winning volley, then the foot sliding into the kitchen. The French federation tightened this rule in September 2026.

Stay slightly back from the line to volley, and brake before you hit, not after.

Try this: Volley with a partner and freeze after each shot. Feet still before the next contact.
