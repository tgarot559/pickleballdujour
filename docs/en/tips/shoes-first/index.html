Tip #24 · Health · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

Shoes before the paddle

In shortPickleball means lateral movement and sudden stops. Running shoes are a sprain waiting to happen.

Get court shoes: tennis or padel outdoors, indoor court shoes inside. Good ankle support and a sole that grips without sticking.

Try this: Check your current soles: if they're smooth or heavily rounded at the sides, replace them before your next session.
