Tip #20 · Mindset · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

One point at a time

In shortOnly the serving team scores: you can lose six rallies without losing a point. Don't panic.

Pickleball's scoring system is your friend in tough moments. As long as your opponent doesn't serve well, they don't score. Focus on the current rally, not the score.

Try this: After each lost point, take one slow breath before resetting. Three seconds, no more.
