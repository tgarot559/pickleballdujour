Tip #23 · Doubles · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

When in doubt, go middle

In shortA ball between the two opponents creates hesitation and cuts the angles they can send back.

Aiming at the sidelines is risky: little margin, and the opponent can reply with an angle. The middle is safe, and it's often where confusion starts on the other side.

Try this: For one game, hit every attacking ball down the middle. Count the errors it causes.
