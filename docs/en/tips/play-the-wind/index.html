Tip #19 · Outdoors · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

Play the wind

In shortWind behind you: play shorter and softer. Wind in your face: you can hit harder, the ball will be slowed.

The pickleball is light and perforated: wind carries it far more than a tennis ball. At every change of ends, recalibrate.

Try this: At the warm-up, hit five dinks each way to feel the wind before the first point.
