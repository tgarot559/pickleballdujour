Tip #12 · Strategy · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

Lob, but rarely

In shortA good lob is beautiful. A short lob is a gift-wrapped smash. Keep it as a surprise.

The lob works against opponents glued to the kitchen line and leaning forward. It fails in wind or when the opponent is already backing up.

Outdoors with the wind behind you, forget it.

Try this: Hit five lobs in practice and note how many truly land behind the opponent's service line.
