Dinking is patience

In shortA dink rally is rarely won by a brilliant shot. It's won when the other side loses patience and pops the ball up.

The point of a dink isn't to win the rally but to give your opponent a ball they can't attack: low, in the kitchen, ideally to their backhand.

Wait for the ball that's too high. That one you can speed up.

Try this: Dink rally with a partner: first to hit outside the kitchen or into the net loses the point. Play to 11.
