Three words that save a doubles game

In short"Mine", "yours", "leave it". In doubles, half the points lost down the middle come from silence.

The ball down the middle is the most dangerous: each player thinks the other has it. Agree before the match. Often, the player with the forehand in the middle takes it.

And shout "leave it" when a ball is going out. Your partner doesn't always have time to judge.

Try this: Play a game where every middle ball must be called out loud. Fault if nobody speaks.
