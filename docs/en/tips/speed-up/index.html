Tip #18 · Strategy · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

Speed up the right ball

In shortA speed-up at the wrong moment comes back faster than it left. Wait for a ball above the net.

In a dink rally, don't speed up a low ball: you'd have to hit upwards, and your opponent just volleys down.

The right ball to speed up arrives at hip height or higher.

Try this: During a dink rally, only speed up balls that clear the top of the net by more than eight inches.
