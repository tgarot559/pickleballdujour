Use the right ball

In shortOutdoor ball indoors: it bounces too much and skids. Indoor ball outdoors: the wind takes it.

Outdoor balls are heavier and harder, with more, smaller holes. Indoor balls are lighter and softer. They have 26 to 40 holes depending on the model.

In sanctioned play, the ball must be on the USA Pickleball approved list.

Try this: If you play in both conditions, keep a tube of each in your bag and label which is which.
