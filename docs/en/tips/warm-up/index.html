Five minutes of warm-up

In shortPickleball injuries mostly hit ankles, calves and shoulders. Five minutes are enough to prevent most of them.

Before playing: brisk walking, high knees, side shuffles, shoulder and wrist rotations. Then a few easy dinks before speeding up.

Lateral movement and sudden stops are what load the body most.

Try this: Before your next session: 1 minute of side shuffles, 1 minute of shoulder rotations, 3 minutes of dinks.
