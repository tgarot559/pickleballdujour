Serve deep, not hard

In shortA serve landing near the baseline pushes the receiver back and buys you time. Depth matters more than pace.

You only get one serve. Missing it hands over the serve. So aim for a big zone about three feet inside the opponent's baseline rather than for the lines.

A deep serve forces the receiver back. Their return will be shorter, and your third shot easier.

Try this: Put a towel three feet inside the far baseline. Ten serves: how many land beyond it?
