Tip #17 · Doubles · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

Move as if you're tied together

In shortIn doubles, picture a ten-foot rope between you and your partner. When one moves, the other follows.

If your partner is pulled wide, slide to the middle to close the gap. If you move up, move up together. A staggered team, one up and one back, opens a highway down the diagonal.

Try this: Play a game where you must keep your partner beside you, never in front or behind.
