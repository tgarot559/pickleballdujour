Tip #15 · Technique · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

One grip for everything

In shortThe "hammer", or continental, grip lets you play forehand, backhand and volley without changing grip.

Hold the paddle like a hammer, edge towards the net. At the net, you'll never have time to switch grips between volleys.

Don't squeeze: on a scale of 1 to 10, stay around 4.

Try this: Play a whole dink session holding the hammer grip, never adjusting it.
