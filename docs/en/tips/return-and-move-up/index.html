Tip #2 · Return · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

After the return, get to the kitchen

In shortThe receiving team has an edge: it can move to the net right after the return. Don't waste it by staying back.

Thanks to the two-bounce rule, the server must let your return bounce. Use that time to move forward. Hit your return deep and slow: it gives you more time to reach the kitchen line.

Stop just behind the line, feet apart, paddle in front.

Try this: Hit ten returns and count your steps to the kitchen line out loud. Goal: be there before the ball comes back over the net.
