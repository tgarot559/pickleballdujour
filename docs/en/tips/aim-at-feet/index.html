Tip #8 · Strategy · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

Aim at the feet, not the head

In shortA ball arriving at a net player's feet is almost impossible to return aggressively.

When you speed up, the temptation is to hit at chest height. But a high ball is easy to volley. A low ball at the shoes forces a defensive half-volley.

Try this: Lay a paddle on the ground in front of your partner's feet and aim at it for ten speed-ups.
