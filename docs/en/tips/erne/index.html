The erne, for bold players

In shortGoing around the kitchen to volley from outside the court is legal, as long as you don't touch the zone.

When an opponent dinks along the sideline, you can jump over the corner of the kitchen, or set up outside the court beside it, to volley. Surprise guaranteed.

Condition: your feet must not touch the kitchen or its lines, before or during the shot.

Try this: In practice, walk slowly past the kitchen five times without a ball to memorise where to put your feet.
