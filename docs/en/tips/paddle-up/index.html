Paddle up, always

In shortAt the net, exchanges are too fast to bring your paddle up from your waist. Keep it in front, at chest height.

It's the number one beginner habit: the paddle drops after every shot. So you're always late on speed-ups.

Ready position: paddle in front of your chest, head slightly up, elbows in front of your body. Come back to it after every shot.

Try this: During a game, ask your partner to shout "paddle" every time yours drops below your waist.
