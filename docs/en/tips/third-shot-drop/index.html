Tip #3 · Strategy · By Thierry Garot, pickleball player

The third shot is the drop

In shortThe serving team is stuck at the back. A drop, a soft ball into the opponent's kitchen, lets it move up without being punished.

Drive your third shot at opponents already at the net and it'll come back at your feet. The drop forces them to play a low ball they can't attack.

Aim just over the net with a soft arc, and take two or three steps forward as soon as it leaves your paddle.

Try this: From the baseline, hit twenty drops into the opponent's kitchen. Count how many bounce there.
