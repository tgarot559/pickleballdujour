Beginner? Go light

In shortA 7 to 8 oz paddle forgives mistakes and protects your elbow. You can move to heavier later.

Heavy paddles add power, but in pickleball control wins points. And a tired arm misses dinks.

Expect €45 to €85 for a good first paddle.

Try this: Before buying, test two paddles of different weights at your club, at least twenty minutes each.
