Pickleball rules, explained simply

In shortFive rules are enough to play: 1) serve underhand, diagonally, past the kitchen; 2) the ball must bounce once on each side before anyone volleys; 3) no volleying while touching the kitchen; 4) only the serving team scores; 5) games go to 11, win by 2.

1. The serve

  • One serve attempt per player: a fault and the serve is lost.
  • The server keeps at least one foot on the ground behind the baseline, within the extension of their court.
  • The motion is upward, the paddle head below the wrist, and contact at or below waist level.
  • The ball goes diagonally and must land past the kitchen. A serve touching the kitchen line is a fault; everywhere else, a ball on the line is in.
  • A serve that clips the net and lands in the correct court is good and played.

The drop serve (ball dropped and hit after the bounce): USA Pickleball exempts it from the motion requirements. The French federation's rules still require contact at or below the waist and an upward motion, and that stricter reading applies in sanctioned French tournaments.

2. The two-bounce rule

After the serve, the receiving team lets the ball bounce. Then the serving team lets the return bounce. Only after that can anyone volley. This rule stops the serving team rushing the net and makes the third shot so important.

3. The kitchen (non-volley zone)

  • You may step into the kitchen at any time. What's forbidden is volleying (hitting before the bounce) while touching the zone or its lines.
  • Momentum counts: volley just before the line and land in it, and it's a fault, even after the rally ends.
  • Touching the kitchen with your paddle during a volley is also a fault.
  • A ball that bounced in the kitchen can be played from inside it.

4. Scoring

Only the serving side scores, one point at a time. Games usually go to 11, win by 2. In doubles, the server calls three numbers before each serve: serving team score, receiving team score, then server number (1 or 2).

  • A doubles game starts at 0-0-2: the first serving team gets only one server on its first turn.
  • After that, each partner serves in turn. When both have lost the serve, it passes to the other team.
  • Even score: serve from the right. Odd score: from the left.

5. Common faults

  • Ball into the net or out of bounds.
  • Volleying before both bounces have happened.
  • Volleying while touching the kitchen, including through momentum.
  • Touching the net or posts during the rally.
  • A ball that hits the net and falls back on your own side.

What changed in 2026

USA Pickleball updates its rulebook every 1 January. The 2026 edition explicitly allows spin put on the ball by the paddle at contact, and gives the point to the hitter when a good ball bounces and then hits a post. Rally scoring (a point on every rally) was tested, with a decision announced for 2027. In France, the federation's own rules apply in sanctioned events; on 1 September 2026 they tightened the kitchen momentum rule.

Frequently asked questions

Can the serve touch the net?

Yes. If it clips the net and lands in the correct court past the kitchen, it is good and played.

Can you step into the kitchen?

Yes, anytime. You just can't volley while touching it. A ball that bounced in the kitchen is played normally.

Why does the score have three numbers?

In doubles, the third number tells which partner is serving: server 1 or server 2.

Why does the game start at 0-0-2?

To offset the advantage of serving first, the starting team gets only one server on its first turn.

Is a ball on the line in?

Yes, everywhere except a serve landing on the kitchen line, which is a fault.
