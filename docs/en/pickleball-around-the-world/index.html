Pickleball around the world

In shortPickleball is still above all an American phenomenon, with 24.3 million US players in 2025. But it's spreading fast: 1.8 million players in Canada in 2026, pro events in Asia and Australia, and a World Cup that drew over 4,000 players from more than 80 countries to Vietnam in 2026.

The pro circuits

The PPA Tour is the main professional circuit. It now runs a fall-to-spring season, August to May, ending with finals for the top 16 men and women. It has expanded to Asia (Hanoi, Kuala Lumpur, Tokyo) and Australia.

Major League Pickleball (MLP) is a team league that takes over in summer. In 2026 it launched a first Nations Cup between regional teams, won by the USA.

DUPR is the international rating system used at many events, including by the French federation to place players.

The 2026 World Cup in Vietnam

It was held in Da Nang from 30 August to 6 September 2026, with more than 4,000 athletes and over 80 national delegations. France fielded its very first national team, months after the French Tennis Federation took official charge of the sport.

Names to know

On the women's side, American Anna Leigh Waters has dominated the tour for several seasons. On the men's side, Ben Johns remains the benchmark, with a new generation of teenagers rising fast. We follow results every week in the tip of the day and the newsletter.

Europe and France

According to the FFT, France is well placed at European level, even if the US remains far ahead. A three-year deal with the US Open now sends 20 French players to Naples, Florida, every year, and brings 20 US amateurs to the French Open.

Frequently asked questions

How many people play pickleball worldwide?

There's no reliable global figure. The US had 24.3 million players in 2025 and Canada 1.8 million in 2026.

Who are the best pickleball players?

Anna Leigh Waters on the women's side and Ben Johns on the men's side are the PPA Tour benchmarks.

Where was the 2026 Pickleball World Cup?

In Da Nang, Vietnam, from 30 August to 6 September 2026, with over 4,000 athletes from more than 80 countries.
