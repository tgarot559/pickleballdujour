About

I'm Thierry Garot. I play pickleball regularly in France, and like many players here I learned by piecing answers together: American videos, forums, half-translated rules.

I built Pickleball du Jour to offer what I wish I'd found when I started: one simple tip a day to try in your next game, and clear, checked guides on rules, gear and tournaments, with a view from France and Europe.

How the site is made

  • Every figure and rule is checked against official sources (French Tennis Federation, USA Pickleball) or the press, with the date checked.
  • Pages are updated as soon as a rule or calendar changes.
  • Illustrations are original.
  • The site is independent: no brand pays for a review.

Get in touch

Spotted a mistake, a club to add, a question for the letter? Message me on LinkedIn. My other projects are on thierrygarot.fr.
