Pickleball tournaments: the 2026-2027 calendar

In shortThe big French events of the 2026-2027 season: the Winter Open in Marcq-en-Barœul on 22-24 January 2027, the French Championships in Chevigny (23-25 April) and Chambéry (7-9 May), and the French Open on 18-20 June 2027. Worldwide, the US Open runs in Naples, Florida, on 17-24 April 2027.

French tournament categories

Since 1 September 2026, the FFT ranks tournaments in five categories, from most accessible to most prestigious: K25, K100, K250, K500 and K1000. The higher the number, the more ranking points. For 2026-2027 the FFT awarded 12 K250 and 4 K500 events; K25 and K100 events are run by clubs, committees and regions. Age groups are U14, U18, Seniors and 50+.

Levels 3.0, 4.0, 5.0

Draws are split by level: 3.0 for first-time competitors, 4.0 intermediate, 5.0 elite. The FFT also uses DUPR, the international rating, to place players.

Entering a tournament in France

All sanctioned tournaments are listed on Ten'Up, the FFT's app, and require an FFT licence. Through a three-year agreement with the US Open, 20 French players are invited to Naples each year, and 20 US Open amateurs to the French Open.

The pro circuits

The PPA Tour now runs from August to May, with finals in May, while the MLP team league takes the summer. The US Open in Naples bills itself as the world's largest tournament, with about 4,000 players. More on pickleball around the world.

Frequently asked questions

When are the 2027 French Championships?

23-25 April 2027 in Chevigny for levels 3.0 and 4.0, and 7-9 May 2027 in Chambéry for 5.0.

What do K100 or K500 mean?

They're French tournament categories. There are five, from K25 to K1000: the higher the number, the more ranking points.

When is the 2027 US Open Pickleball Championships?

17-24 April 2027 in Naples, Florida.

Do I need a licence to play a tournament in France?

Yes, an FFT licence is required for sanctioned tournaments.
