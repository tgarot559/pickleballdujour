Where to play pickleball in France

In shortThe easiest way is the interactive club map on the French Tennis Federation (FFT) website, listing clubs that offer pickleball. Over 1,000 FFT tennis clubs offered it in late 2026. There are also pickleball-only centres, such as Pickle Arena in Bordeaux, the first of its kind in France.

Find a club in three minutes

  1. The FFT club map: on the pickleball page of fft.fr, an interactive map shows clubs offering the sport.
  2. Ten'Up: the FFT app for booking courts and finding sanctioned tournaments.
  3. Your local tennis or padel club: ask, even if nothing is advertised. Many have opened pickleball slots quietly.
  4. The town hall: more and more towns mark courts in sports halls and outdoor areas.

Places that matter

  • Pickle Arena, Bordeaux: opened in August 2024, France's first venue dedicated entirely to pickleball, with three indoor resin courts. A similar centre has since opened in Lyon.
  • Pikkel, Chambéry: hosts the 5.0 French Championships, 7-9 May 2027.
  • Chevigny Tennis Club (Burgundy): hosts the 3.0 and 4.0 French Championships, 23-25 April 2027.
  • Niort: the local FFT committee runs a pickleball week on 25-30 May 2027, with a K100 tournament on 29-30 May.

Visiting France?

If you're travelling, book through Ten'Up or contact clubs directly: many welcome visitors for a session. Bring your own paddle, and outdoor balls if you plan to play outside.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a pickleball court in France?

Use the interactive club map on fft.fr or the Ten'Up app, and ask your local tennis or padel club.

How many clubs offer pickleball in France?

Over 1,000 FFT tennis clubs in late 2026, according to the federation.

Are there pickleball-only venues in France?

Yes. Pickle Arena in Bordeaux, opened in August 2024, was the first. Others followed, including in Lyon.
