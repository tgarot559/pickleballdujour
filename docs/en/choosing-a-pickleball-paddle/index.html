How to choose a pickleball paddle

In shortFor beginners, pick a light paddle (7 to 8 oz, 200 to 230 g) with a fibreglass face and a wide shape, around €45 to €85. Pricier carbon paddles (€140 to €180) add control and spin, but only really pay off from an intermediate level.

The four things that matter

1. Weight

A light paddle (7 to 8 oz, 200 to 230 g) is quick in the hands and easy on the arm: right for beginners and net play. A heavier one, above 8.5 oz (240 g), adds power but needs a conditioned arm.

2. Face material

Fibreglass is softer and more forgiving, with easy power. Carbon is stiffer, more precise and grips the ball for spin. Wooden paddles exist but are heavy, best kept for very occasional play.

3. Core

Most paddles have a polymer (polypropylene) honeycomb core. Thicker means more stable and controlled; thinner means livelier.

4. Shape

A wide, rounded shape gives a big hitting area: perfect for beginners. An elongated shape adds reach and swing speed, but the sweet spot is smaller.

What the rules say

In sanctioned play, a paddle can't exceed 17 inches (43.18 cm) in length, and length plus width can't exceed 24 inches (60.96 cm). It must appear on the USA Pickleball (or UPA) approved list. No limits for casual play.

Ranges checked in late November 2026 at major French retailers (Decathlon, specialist shops). Examples: Head Attitude Core 2026 around €45, Joola from about €66, Wilson Fierce Max V2 around €85, Kuikma React (Decathlon) at €69.99, Wilson Blaze SF Pro 13 around €140. Prices change often.

Which brand?

  • Decathlon (Kuikma): the smart way to start or kit out a family.
  • Head: a wide, affordable beginner range and solid advanced models.
  • Joola: used by many top American pros, with sought-after carbon models.
  • Wilson: versatile, easy-to-use paddles.
  • Selkirk, Babolat, Adidas: worth a look once your game settles.

My advice: try before you buy. Most clubs have loan paddles, and two sessions tell you whether you prefer power or control.

Frequently asked questions

What paddle weight is best for beginners?

Around 7 to 8 oz (200 to 230 g). Easy to handle, forgiving, and gentler on the arm and elbow.

Carbon or fibreglass?

Fibreglass to start: more forgiving and cheaper. Carbon when you want control and spin, from an intermediate level.

How much does a good paddle cost?

About €45 to €85 for beginners, €70 to €120 for intermediates and €140 to €180 for high-end models.

Are Decathlon paddles any good?

Yes for beginners: good value and easy to find. Regular players often move to a specialist brand later.
